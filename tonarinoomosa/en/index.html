Tonari no Omosa

Stones, weight and quiet logic

Preparing for release

Place one stone. Its neighbours grow heavier. Tonari no Omosa is a logic puzzle about connections: arrange stones to match the weight of every row and column. HOW TO PLAY A stone alone weighs 1. Each stone directly above, below, left or right adds 1 to its weight. Diagonals do not count. The numbers on the right and below show “current total / target”. Match them all to solve the puzzle. Tap to place or remove stones. Use “Mark empty” to mark a cell with ×. Cells with a dot cannot be changed. FROM SMALL BOARDS TO DEEPER CHALLENGES • All 60 basic puzzles on 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5 boards are free. • Hard, Expert and Master packs contain 30 puzzles each. The first 3 in each pack are also free. • Play 69 of the 150 puzzles for free. Buying a pack unlocks its other 27 puzzles. • There is no timer. Use undo, empty marks and hints at your own pace. MORE THAN SOLVING Enjoy gentle stone sounds and slowly moving light. Solve puzzles to unlock new colours. In Free play, make shapes and explore sounds without a right answer. Sound, vibration and movement can be adjusted in Settings. Today’s puzzle is selected from the 60 basic puzzles and repeats on a 60-day cycle. OPTIONAL PURCHASES Puzzle packs, ad removal, all puzzles, and the ad-free + all puzzles bundle are one-time purchases. There are no subscriptions. “All puzzles” covers the 150 puzzles currently included. Bundles containing content you already own cannot be purchased. iPhone and Android purchases are separate. Restore purchases with the same account in the same store. Puzzles can be played offline. Ads, purchases and restoring purchases require an internet connection. Progress is saved on your device and does not automatically sync between devices.
