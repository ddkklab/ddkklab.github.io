How to play

Find the balance

01

A stone on its own weighs 1. Each stone directly above, below, left or right adds 1 to its weight. Diagonals do not count.

02

The numbers on the right and below show “current total / target”. Match them all to solve the puzzle. A ✓ marks each match.

03

Tap to place or remove a stone. Use “Mark empty” to add × marks. Cells with a dot cannot be changed.
