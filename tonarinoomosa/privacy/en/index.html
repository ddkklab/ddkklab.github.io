Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Preparing for release — ddkklab

Information saved on your device

We save puzzle progress, placed stones, empty marks, solved puzzles, daily puzzle records, sound, vibration, motion and colour settings, and verified purchase access on your device. There is no separate app account or service that sends puzzle progress to our servers.

Advertising and Google services

The app is designed to use Google AdMob and its consent service. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and consent management. Non-personalised ads still involve data processing. The app does not send puzzle progress to the ad service.

Consent and preferences

Where required, consent is checked before requesting ads. If ad preferences are available in Settings, you can change them there. This is separate from Apple’s tracking permission. Ads may be unavailable depending on consent and serving conditions.

Purchases and restoration

Apple or Google handles payments and transaction records. The app verifies purchase access to unlock and restore content. We do not receive card numbers or store passwords. iPhone and Android purchases are separate. Restoring purchases does not restore puzzle progress.

Storage and deletion

Progress does not automatically sync to other devices. App deletion or device failure may cause progress loss. OS backups may include app data. Deleting the app does not necessarily erase store, advertising or backup records. Third parties retain data under their own policies.

Contact

We use your email and message to answer questions and investigate issues. We keep them for as long as needed for support; contact us about deletion. Do not send passwords or card numbers.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, without our own advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may process access information under its policy. We update the revision date when this notice changes.
