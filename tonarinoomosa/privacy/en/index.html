Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Information saved on your device

Your device saves puzzle progress, stones and marks, solved puzzles, daily records, settings and purchased items. There is no separate game account, and puzzle progress is not sent to our servers.

Advertising and Google services

Ads use Google AdMob. Google may handle information identifying your device, the approximate region indicated by your internet connection, ad and app interactions, and information about errors and performance. This supports advertising, measurement, analysis, fraud prevention and privacy choices. Ads that are not based on your interests still involve this information. Puzzle progress is not sent to the ad service.

Consent and preferences

Where required, we ask about the use of information before showing ads. If ad choices appear in Settings, you can change them there. You can also review app permissions in your device’s privacy settings. Ads may not appear depending on your choices and connection.

Purchases and restoration

Apple or Google handles payments and purchase records. The app checks those records to make purchased items available. We do not receive card numbers or store passwords. iPhone and Android purchases are separate. Restoring purchases does not restore puzzle progress.

Storage and deletion

Progress does not automatically sync to other devices. App deletion or device failure may cause progress loss. Device backups may include app data. Deleting the app does not necessarily erase store, advertising or backup records. Third parties retain data under their own policies.

Contact

We use your email and message to answer questions and investigate issues. We keep them for as long as needed for support; contact us about deletion. Do not send passwords or card numbers.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, without our own advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may process access information under its policy. We update the revision date when this notice changes.
