Common questions

My purchase is not available

Restore purchases with the same account in the same store used for the original purchase.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Progress is saved on your device and does not move automatically to another device. Restoring purchases brings back purchased items, not progress.

How do I change sound and vibration?

Open Settings to change sound, vibration and motion.
