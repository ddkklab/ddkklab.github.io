Preparing for release

Weave a civilization from one life.

Begin with one person. Watch residents gather, explore, build homes and raise the next generation in a peaceful civilization simulation.

Support for twelve languages and an Android edition is in preparation for future releases. The currently submitted iOS version is Japanese. Check the store for availability and supported devices. No Android release date is announced.

ERA 1

Primitive Era

Discover nature, make tools and settle beside the river.

ERA 2

Farming Era

Grow grain, store harvests and develop a farming village.

ERA 3

Ancient Civilization

Work with clay and copper, build workshops and trade.

ERA 4

Medieval & Early Modern

Develop watermills, stone homes, guilds and a city of learning.

A village that grows with its people

Residents choose work, eat and rest, grow from babies into adults and pass life to a new generation. Their clothes and appearance change with the era. Research, buildings and population goals open new chapters and expand the land.

Play at your own pace

The basic game works offline with no account or developer-run backend. Village life simulates up to 8 hours away. Started research and building timers advance separately while the app is closed.

Optional ways to save time

Research and building each start with 2 free slots. Separate one-time purchases add one slot to each, up to 3. Consumable packs contain 6 tickets of 1, 8 or 24 hours, usable on selected research or building projects. See the purchase screen for local prices.

Rewarded ads are optional: a verified reward halves the selected project’s remaining time, up to 1 hour. There are no forced ads or persistent banners. Ads, purchases and purchase restoration require an internet connection.

Four eras, twelve chapters

The current release scope runs through the medieval and early modern era, with three chapters per era. Later eras and update dates are not guaranteed.
