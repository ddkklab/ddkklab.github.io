Privacy policy

Updated November 2, 2026. This policy explains information handling for YOYOTSUMUGI, provided by ddkklab. The app is preparing for release.

Game information on your device

Residents, resources, history, research, game time, ad-reward deduplication records and sound settings are stored on your device. There is no developer account system or developer-run backend, and game progress is not sent to our servers. We do not record your microphone or request precise GPS location. OS backups follow your device settings.

Advertising and third parties

Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Consent is managed with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean that no data is processed.

Google’s SDKs may process approximate location derived from IP addresses, device identifiers, product interactions, advertising data, crash data, performance data and other diagnostics. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and consent management. We do not send village saves or purchase entitlements to the ad SDK.

Tracking and advertising choices

When you first choose to use ads, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission may be requested as needed. If allowed, device identifiers may be associated with other companies’ data for advertising measurement. Device identifiers are treated as data used for tracking. You can continue the basic game if you decline. Ads are handled only when consent and delivery conditions permit.

ATT and regional advertising consent are separate. Change tracking permission in iOS Settings. When required, regional consent can be reviewed through Ad privacy settings in the app. You can choose not to watch ads.

Apple StoreKit purchases

Extra research and building slots are non-consumable purchases; time-skip packs are consumable purchases. Verified transactions, entitlements and ticket grant/use/revocation records are kept in the device Keychain to manage access, restore extra slots, maintain balances and prevent duplicate grants. Consumable tickets are not granted again through Restore purchases, and transfer after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed.

We do not receive card numbers or Apple Account passwords. Purchase data is not sent to our own servers or the ad SDK. Apple’s policies apply to payment and transaction records it manages.

Retention and deletion

Saves are local. Deleting the app, device failure or changing devices can cause loss of progress. Restoring purchases does not restore village saves. OS backups, Keychain entries and Apple purchase records may remain after deleting the app. Third-party retention and deletion follow each provider’s policies; we cannot delete all of their records.

Contact information

Contact ddkklab at support.ddkklab@gmail.com. Email addresses, messages and attachments you send are used to answer inquiries and investigate problems. We retain them as needed for that purpose and remove information no longer needed. Contact us about retention or deletion requests. Do not send passwords, card numbers, full receipts or entire save files.

International processing and this website

Google, Apple and GitHub operate internationally. Information they process may be handled outside your country under their policies, including their transfer safeguards and privacy-rights procedures. Your applicable privacy choices depend on your location. See their policies linked below.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting access information may be processed under GitHub’s privacy statement. Services opened from ads have their own terms.

Policy changes

We review this policy when features or providers change and publish the update date here. Material changes will be announced on the website or in the app, with any required consent process.
