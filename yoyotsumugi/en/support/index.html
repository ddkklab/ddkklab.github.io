Support

The app is preparing for release. These FAQs describe the release design; check your supplied app version for final screens and availability.

Where do I begin?

Open the village rating or Requirements to see the research, buildings and population needed for the next chapter or era. Find companions in Residents starts exploration. You can also order gathering for materials missing from research or building.

Do I need an account or internet?

The basic game works offline and requires no account. Ads, purchases and restoring purchases require internet.

Where is my save?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app, a device fault or changing devices may lose it. Contact support before deleting the app to troubleshoot. Restoring purchases does not restore village progress.

How do extra slots work?

Research and building each have 2 free slots. Separate one-time purchases add 1 slot each, up to 3. Restore purchases in Settings using the same Apple Account and an internet connection. If a purchase is pending, check its Apple status rather than purchasing repeatedly.

How do time-skip tickets work?

Each pack contains 6 tickets of 1, 8 or 24 hours. Open Speed up on active research or building, select a ticket and use it individually. Excess time is not carried over. Unused tickets do not expire. Balances are local; consumables are not reissued by Restore purchases. Transfer after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed.

What do rewarded ads do?

Choose an ad from Speed up on an active research or building project. A verified reward halves remaining time, up to 1 hour. The shared limit is 10 per day, 5 minutes apart, resetting at midnight Japan time (15:00 UTC). Ad availability is not guaranteed.

An ad is unavailable or its reward failed to save.

Normal research and building can continue. If a reward-save retry is offered, retry saving before watching another ad.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Use the ad’s information controls where available and send the date, time, content and app version to support. You do not need to click the ad.

Does iPhone Duo support apply to my version?

Adaptive layouts for opening, folding and rotating are being prepared for the next update. A submitted older version does not gain these changes automatically. Check the App Store version notes.
