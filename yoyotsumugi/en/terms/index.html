Use & purchases

Availability

YOYOTSUMUGI is preparing for release. The release covers the primitive, farming, ancient, medieval and early modern eras. Release dates and future updates are not promised. Your App Store and supplied app version define availability and supported devices.

Purchases and ads

The basic game is designed to be playable for free. Separate non-consumable purchases add research and building slots. Consumable packs provide 6 tickets of 1, 8 or 24 hours. Rewarded ads are optional. Check the purchase confirmation for local prices. Apple’s procedures and applicable terms govern purchases and refunds.

Tickets and saved progress

Tickets are used individually on a selected project; time beyond the remaining duration is not carried over. Unused tickets do not expire. Balances stay on the device. Consumables are not reissued by restoring purchases, and transfer after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed. Restoring extra slots does not restore village saves.

Your rights

Apple’s standard app license applies where provided through the App Store. This information does not limit mandatory consumer rights under applicable law.
